Faith from the Philippines: Starting Her Nursing Apprenticeship in Hesse

Faith has started her training as a registered nurse at a hospital in Hesse. How she prepared, what her first weeks were like and what she would tell others.

Heinz Personnel Solutions · Personal Story

Faith comes from the Philippines. Since this autumn, she has been training as a registered nurse at a hospital in Hesse. Heinz accompanied her along the way, from language school to her first day on the ward. Here she tells her story.

Why nursing, why Germany?

"I have wanted to work in nursing for a long time. In Germany, I learn the profession from the ground up and already earn money during my training. For me, it was the best chance."

Learning German before departure

Before apprentices come to Germany with Heinz, they learn German up to B2. Faith did too.

"Learning German was really hard at first, especially the grammar and the long words. But a little every day paid off. When I passed the B2 exam, I was incredibly proud."

Arriving in Hesse

Faith travelled to Germany with a large group, accompanied by the Heinz team. Flat, registration with the city and bank account: we took the first steps together.

"I was very nervous. But I wasn't alone: we travelled as a big group, and Heinz accompanied us, picked us up and showed me everything. The hospital also takes great care of us. After a few days, I already felt a little bit at home."

The first weeks at the hospital

"The team welcomed me very warmly. At first everything was new: the routines, the documentation and how directly people speak here. My practical instructor explains a lot and is always patient when I ask questions."

Vocational school in German

"There are many technical terms in class. I write everything down and study in the evening. It is tiring, but I understand more every day, and with B2 I can keep up well."

What helped her most

"What helped me most were the people: my colleagues on the ward, the other apprentices and the Heinz team. Whenever I have a question, I always know whom to call."

Her advice to others

"Learn German properly, not just for the exam. And don't be afraid to ask questions. At first everything is new, but it gets easier every day."

What Faith's path shows employers

Faith is trained under the German Nursing Professions Act from day one, at the hospital and at a German nursing school. There is no recognition procedure for a foreign qualification. After three years, she knows the processes, the team and the patients of her hospital. With her qualification, she can stay as a skilled worker.
