What to expect

Learn. Earn. Stay.

01

A German qualification

You learn at the company and at a vocational school. Your qualification is valid in Germany without a recognition procedure.

02

Paid from day one

You receive training pay from the first month. For the visa it has to cover your living costs. We check this with the company in advance.

03

Stay afterwards

Once qualified, you can work in Germany as a skilled worker, often at your training company. After two years of work, a permanent settlement permit is possible if further requirements are met.

Source on the settlement permit: Section 18c(1) Residence Act, Make it in Germany.

Your direction

Many occupations. One path.

Tell us what interests you and what you are good at. We find the company that suits you. Depending on the occupation, training usually takes two to three and a half years.

Your preferred occupation is a different one? Get in touch. We place trainees in many other occupations too.

What you bring

Starting well prepared.

  • We recommend 12 years of school.

    This way you start well prepared for vocational school and your German course. We look at your certificates together with you.

  • Willingness to learn German.

    We organise your German course up to B2. Your effort decides how fast you progress.

  • Motivation for an occupation and for Germany.

    Vocational training is a big step. If you know why you are taking it, you settle in well.

Your path

Step by step to Germany.

How long it takes until you start depends mainly on how quickly you learn German. We support you at every step.

01
Application and getting to know you

You apply, and we get to know you personally. We talk about your wishes and the right occupation.

02
German up to B2

Before entry you learn German up to B2. We organise the course, because vocational school is taught in German.

03
Training place and visa

We match you with a suitable company and take care of your visa applications.

04
Arrival

We pick you up and help with finding housing, dealing with authorities and opening a bank account.

05
Support until you qualify

We stay available throughout your training, for you and for your company.

Is nursing training right for you? Find out.

Are you interested in nursing training? This self-test helps you decide.

The Career Orientation Decision Compass was made for this, by the quality association Faire Anwerbung Pflege Deutschland, which also awards the RAL quality mark. The self-test is aimed at young people from third countries considering nursing training, and helps you honestly work out whether this path suits you.

19

topic areas show what the training really involves: from pay and living costs to shift work, learning German and homesickness.

60

questions in about 15 minutes on the nursing profession, on life in a foreign country and on what this decision means for you personally.

11

languages to choose from, including Arabic, Vietnamese, Ukrainian and Farsi.

PDF

The result comes without a score. It shows where you feel sure and where questions remain. Save it and talk it through at your own pace.

Go to the Decision Compass Discuss your result with us

The self-test is anonymous and non-binding. According to the quality association, answers are not stored, not passed on and have no influence on placement decisions.
