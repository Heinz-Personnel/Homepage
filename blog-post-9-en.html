CPR training: preparation that goes beyond language

Preparation is more than language and recognition. Practical training like CPR is just as much a part of it for us.

Heinz Personnel Solutions · Insights

Professional preparation doesn't end with language and paperwork. In this photo, a group of our candidates stands together after a CPR training session, surrounded by training mannequins, on a rainy day that still worked out for a group photo under an umbrella.

Why practical training is part of preparation

Resuscitation, first aid, confidently handling emergency situations: these are skills that can be needed at any moment in everyday nursing work, no matter which country someone trained in. Anyone who has already practiced these procedures before departure walks into their first day of work at a German organization with more confidence.

Preparation means more than memorizing vocabulary

Language courses and recognition procedures rightly take center stage when it comes to preparing international nursing professionals. But moments like this matter just as much: practical training that builds professional confidence, and that also brings people together, like on this day outdoors, together under umbrellas.

A team, before it even begins

Many of the candidates in this photo already know each other well long before departure, from shared language classes, from training sessions like this one. What often looks in Germany like a random group of freshly arrived professionals is, in reality, the result of months of preparing together.
