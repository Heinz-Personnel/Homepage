The shortage remains. International professionals are the answer.

According to Germany's Federal Employment Agency, 390,000 nursing professionals will retire over the next ten years. The German labour market cannot close that gap alone. We can: with professionals from the Philippines and India, matched precisely rather than placed in bulk, with a clear focus on integration so your facility's investment pays off long term.

For employers

Reliable, long-term staffing instead of recurring searches. A digitally supported process with clear structure and fallback cover.

For professionals

Placement costs nursing professionals absolutely nothing. No fees, no repayment schemes. We work strictly under the RAL quality mark for fair recruitment.

For the collaboration

We work according to a scientific model that has proven itself in practice. Everyone benefits, and lastingly so.

We don't work alone.

We are part of several networks and partnerships, which keeps us close to the latest developments and technology in the market.

bvifg

As a member of the Federal Association for International Skilled Worker Recruitment, we help shape shared standards for fair labour migration.

Ankaadia

The SaaS platform through which we run our entire placement process digitally and transparently.

Lingoda

High-quality online language teaching and recognition courses, preparing our candidates for German with native-speaking teachers.

GAPA

GAPA awards the RAL quality mark "Fair Recruitment in Nursing, Germany" and safeguards fair, ethical and transparent recruitment of international nursing professionals.
