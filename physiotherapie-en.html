Your way

Six steps to your license.

Documents and German classes run in parallel. We guide every step and stay available until everyday life works.

01

Selection

We check your degree, your PRC license and your experience, and we talk to every candidate in person.

02

Documents

about 1 to 2 months, alongside your German classes

Degree certificate, PRC license, proof of clinical hours, apostille (the Philippines joined the Apostille Convention in 2019), certified translations, police clearance certificate and a medical certificate. We walk you through the list until everything is complete.

03

German

depends on your starting level

A2 for the visa, B2 for your full license. We organise your German course.

04

Recognition

up to 4 months, depending on the federal state

The application goes to the responsible state authority and can be filed from abroad. Depending on your degree, the result is equivalence or a compensation measure: a knowledge test or an adaptation course.

Official portal: Recognition in Germany

05

Visa and arrival

fast-track skilled worker procedure

We set up the fast-track procedure with your employer: advance approval, a visa appointment within 3 weeks, and usually a decision within another 3 weeks. A familiar face waits for you at the airport.

06

Exam and license

after arrival

We support your exam preparation and stay available until your license is issued and everyday life works.

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Do I pay Heinz to find me a job?

No. You never pay a placement fee to Heinz.

Is my degree recognised in Germany?

The German authority compares your degree with the German training. If there are major differences, you take a knowledge test or an adaptation course. Then you receive your professional license.

What German level do I need?

A2 to apply for the visa, usually B2 for your professional license.

How long does it take?

About 6 months until you arrive in Germany, if you start with German at A2 and your documents are complete. Reaching your full professional license takes longer. How long depends on the federal state, the authority and the exam dates.

Can I work as a physical therapist right away?

No. You need permission to use the professional title first. What is possible until then, we clarify with you case by case before you travel.
