No detours

What you should know beforehand.

  • The pay has to be enough.

    For the visa, living costs must be covered, currently with around €1,048 gross per month. The statutory minimum training pay in the first year is €724. We work out together how to cover the difference before the start.

  • Vocational school in German is demanding.

    That is why we aim for B2 and provide a fixed contact person when things get difficult.

  • You need authorisation to train.

    Most companies already have it. If not, your chamber can help.

Frequently asked

Short answers.

What does the placement cost?

Nothing for your trainees, we work on the employer-pays principle. For your company: payment only on success, 50% after a successful placement and 50% after the start of training. We discuss the exact terms openly in our first conversation.

How much work is this for us?

You provide the training, we take care of the rest: selection, language training, visa, entry, finding housing and dealing with the authorities. The training contract, the interviews with candidates and the training in your company stay with you.

Where do the trainees come from?

Our trainees come from Asia.

How good is their German?

Our trainees bring German at B2 level, certified before entry. The training visa legally requires B1. We aim higher because vocational school lessons are held in German.

How long until training starts?

From selection to the start of training, plan for around 3 to 6 months.

What if someone drops out?

If the trainee resigns on their own within the first 18 months, we take over the new search completely, at no extra cost to you.

Which skilled trades do you place trainees in?

For example carpentry and other trades. Feel free to ask us about your occupation.

What happens after the training?

With the qualification, your trainee can stay with you as a skilled worker. The residence permit is adjusted for this, and we support the process.
